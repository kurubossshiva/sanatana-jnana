About Sanatana Jnana

Sanatana Jnana is an independent educational initiative intended to make spiritual and philosophical learning more accessible in Telugu, English and Hindi.

Our goal is to present source verses, transliteration, word meanings, introductory explanations and practical reflections while respecting the diversity of Hindu traditions.

This is an early-stage website. Content will be expanded and reviewed over time. Please use the Contact page to share corrections or suggestions.
