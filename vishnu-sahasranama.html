LEARNING COLLECTION • 01

Vishnu Sahasranama

Learn one verse at a time. The complete original Sanskrit verse is shown first; choose a language for its pronunciation guide and explanation.

COMPLETE SANSKRIT VERSE • 1
विश्वं विष्णुर्वषट्कारो भूतभव्यभवत्प्रभुः ।
भूतकृद्भूतभृद्भावो भूतात्मा भूतभावनः ॥

The original Sanskrit verse remains unchanged in every language.

PRONUNCIATION GUIDE

Read the verse aloud

Viśvaṁ Viṣṇur vaṣaṭkāro bhūta-bhavya-bhavat-prabhuḥ;
Bhūtakṛd bhūtabhṛd bhāvo bhūtātmā bhūta-bhāvanaḥ.

WORD-BY-WORD STUDY

Understand each name

1. Viśvam (విశ్వమ్)

The universe; all that exists.

2. Viṣṇuḥ (విష్ణుః)

The all-pervading one.

3. Vaṣaṭkāraḥ (వషట్కారః)

The sacred invocation used in Vedic offerings.

4. Bhūta-bhavya-bhavat-prabhuḥ

The Lord of the past, future and present.

5. Bhūtakṛt (భూతకృత్)

The creator or originator of beings.

6. Bhūtabhṛt (భూతభృత్)

The supporter and sustainer of beings.

7. Bhāvaḥ (భావః)

Pure existence; the essential state of being.

8. Bhūtātmā (భూతాత్మా)

The inner Self of all beings.

9. Bhūta-bhāvanaḥ (భూతభావనః)

The one who nourishes and develops all beings.

SIMPLE MEANING

What does the verse say?

The verse praises Vishnu as the universe, the all-pervading presence, the Lord of all time, the creator and sustainer of beings, and the inner Self who nourishes all life.

GO DEEPER

The deeper spiritual meaning

The verse presents the Divine not as distant from creation, but as its source, support and inner presence. It invites us to look at the world with reverence and to recognise the connectedness of life.

REFLECTION • EVERYDAY LIFE

A thought to carry with you

If we remember that all beings are supported by the same Divine reality, we may become more patient, compassionate and respectful towards people, animals and nature.
