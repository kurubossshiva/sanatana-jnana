Vishnu Sahasranama

A traditional hymn containing a thousand names of Lord Vishnu. This starter page introduces the opening verse. More names and lessons can be added progressively.

OPENING VERSE

श्लोकः १

विश्वं विष्णुर्वषट्कारो भूतभव्यभवत्प्रभुः ।
भूतकृद्भूतभृद्भावो भूतात्मा भूतभावनः ॥

Transliteration: Viśvaṁ Viṣṇur vaṣaṭkāro bhūta-bhavya-bhavat-prabhuḥ; Bhūtakṛd bhūtabhṛd bhāvo bhūtātmā bhūta-bhāvanaḥ.

Introductory meaning: The verse praises Vishnu as the universe, the sustaining presence, the source and support of beings, and the inner Self of all.

Reflection: The name Viśvam invites us to see the universe as sacred and interconnected. How might that change the way we treat other living beings?

Note: This is a concise introductory explanation, not a substitute for a traditional commentary. Word segmentation and interpretation can vary by edition and tradition.
