OPENING VERSE • 1
विश्वं विष्णुर्वषट्कारो भूतभव्यभवत्प्रभुः ।
भूतकृद्भूतभृद्भावो भूतात्मा भूतभावनः ॥

A traditional opening verse that praises Vishnu as the universe, its sustaining presence, the source of beings and the inner Self of all.

Meaning is an introductory summary; translations and commentarial traditions may vary.
WISDOM IN ACTION

A teaching from the Gita

A timeless reminder to act with care while letting go of attachment to results.

BHAGAVAD GITA • 2.47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।

Your responsibility is to action, not to claim control over its results. This verse invites sincere effort without becoming consumed by outcomes.
