CHAPTER 2 • VERSE 47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

Introductory meaning: You have a claim to action alone, never to its fruits. Do not make the fruits of action your motive, nor become attached to inaction.

Practical reflection: Prepare sincerely for an examination, do your work with care, and learn from the outcome. Your effort is within your responsibility; every external result is not fully within your control.

Meaning is introductory. Study the full verse and traditional commentaries for deeper understanding.
